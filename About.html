Hi, I’m Jessica.

Sewing is one of my hobbies, and this website gives me a way to share it while learning something new myself.

Why I chose sewing

I wanted my website to focus on something I enjoy. Sewing gives me a chance to work with my hands and turn a simple piece of fabric into something useful.

This site is a starting point for someone who wants to try sewing but isn’t sure what to buy or make first. I kept the supply list small and chose a project that gives beginners a chance to practice straight stitches.

Learning as I go

I created this website for a course project using HTML and CSS. Organizing the pages and project documents helps me practice breaking a bigger task into smaller steps.

There is still more to learn, both in sewing and in building websites. For now, my goal is to keep things useful, clear, and easy to follow.
