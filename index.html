You don’t need a room full of supplies to start sewing. A few basic tools, a simple project, and a little patience are a good place to begin.

Start with a small sewing kit

Fabric and thread

Woven cotton is a manageable choice for a first project. Pick matching all-purpose thread and wash and dry your fabric before cutting.

Tools for measuring

Use a measuring tape or ruler, fabric scissors, and washable fabric chalk. Accurate measurements make the sewing part easier.

Tools for stitching

Gather needles, pins or clips, and a seam ripper. For machine sewing, read your machine’s manual to learn how to thread it and choose a needle.

A simple fabric bookmark

Practice straight seams with two small pieces of fabric. Sew by hand or use a sewing machine.

You’ll need: two cotton rectangles measuring 3 × 9 inches, thread, scissors, pins or clips, and a needle or sewing machine.

Keep fingers away from the machine needle. Follow your machine’s manual, and use care with scissors and a hot iron.

  1. Prepare the fabric. Cut two matching rectangles. Place them with the right sides (the sides you want to show) facing each other.
  2. Sew the edges. Stitch ¼ inch from the edge, leaving a 2-inch opening along one long side. Secure the beginning and end of your stitching.
  3. Turn it right side out. Carefully trim excess fabric at the corners without cutting the stitches. Turn through the opening and gently shape the corners.
  4. Finish your bookmark. Fold the opening’s edges inward by ¼ inch and press flat. Stitch close to the edge all the way around to close the opening.

Give yourself room to learn

  • Try a few stitches on scrap fabric before sewing your project.
  • Check your measurements before making a cut.
  • Slow down at corners and keep your seam allowance consistent.
  • A seam ripper is part of the process. It’s okay to try a seam again.
